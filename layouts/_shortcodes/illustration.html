{{- /* illustration — one of the page's SVG illustrations, from assets/img//. Two placements: default floated right beside the paragraphs that follow it. From lg the figure hangs in the margin to the right of the reading column, so the text keeps its full measure; at md it floats inside the column and the text wraps; on phones it stacks, centred, above the text. wide a figure of its own across the whole reading column, for a diagram too wide to sit beside text. An , not inlined: the artwork has its lettering drawn as paths, so the alt text carries the words, and the files stay out of the HTML. Params: src (required) file basename under assets/img// alt (required) what the illustration says width (floated only) its width beside the text, e.g. "10rem". Each piece has its own shape, so this is set per illustration; phones get three quarters of it, and a caption may run wider than the art. wide "true" for the full-column placement An optional caption goes between the tags (Markdown). */ -}} {{- $src := .Get "src" -}} {{- $alt := .Get "alt" -}} {{- if or (not $src) (not $alt) -}} {{- errorf "illustration: 'src' and 'alt' are required (%s)" .Position -}} {{- end -}} {{- $path := printf "img/%s/%s.svg" .Page.Section $src -}} {{- $img := resources.Get $path -}} {{- if not $img -}} {{- errorf "illustration: no assets/%s (%s)" $path .Position -}} {{- end -}} {{- $img = $img | minify | fingerprint -}} {{- $wide := eq (.Get "wide") "true" -}} {{- $caption := "" -}} {{- with .Inner -}} {{- $caption = . | strings.TrimSpace | $.Page.RenderString -}} {{- end -}} {{- if $wide -}} {{- else -}} {{- $width := .Get "width" -}} {{- if not $width -}} {{- errorf "illustration: a floated illustration needs 'width' (%s)" .Position -}} {{- end -}} {{/* The lg margin hang: a right margin of minus (width + left margin) takes the float's whole box out of the line box, so the text runs full measure and the figure sits in the gutter beside it. */}} {{- end -}}
