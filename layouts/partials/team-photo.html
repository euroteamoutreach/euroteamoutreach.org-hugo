Until a photo is on Cloudinary (`photo.id` empty), it renders a blank warm-100 frame of the same shape and warns at build time, so the layout can be reviewed before the photos land without anything shipping silently. The frame takes the photo's own shape unless `class` sets an aspect ratio. "alt" $photo.alt "sizes" $.sizes "width" $photo.width "height" $photo.height "loading" $.loading "class" (printf "bg-warm-100 %s" $.class) ) }} {{- else -}} {{- warnf "team-photo: %s has no photo.id yet; rendering a placeholder" $page.File.Path -}}
{{- end -}}
