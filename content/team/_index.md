Euro Team Outreach is a handful of missionary families who have given their lives to one calling — making Jesus Christ known in Ukraine. We serve hand in hand with Ukrainian believers and local churches, supported by a community of churches and partners who pray, give, and go alongside us.

When you stand with ETO, you're standing with real people — families who packed up, moved across the world, and stayed, through peace and through war.
